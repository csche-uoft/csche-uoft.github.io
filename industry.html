Industry Mentorship

Connect with professionals and upper-year students to navigate your career path, refine your resume, and prepare for PEY Co-op.

PEY-Student Mixer

The PEY Student Mixer allows students considering placements to network with upper-year students who have returned from or are currently completing a 12-16 month PEY work term.

Many students are nervous about opportunities and unsure what to expect. This event provides first-hand insights into internships across various sectors, helping to demystify the process.

Resume and Interview Drop-Ins

To supplement the Engineering Career Centre, we hold resume and interview workshops tailored to student demand.

Our mentors help mentees polish their resumes, prepare for interviews, and succeed with their applications. It's the perfect way to get personalized feedback before deadlines.

Professional Experience Mentorship Program

This year-long initiative builds meaningful relationships between undergraduate students interested in the PEY program and those who have already completed it.

The focus is to aid primarily second and third-year students throughout the application process, easing the transition between academia and industry.
