Explore the clusters here: ​engineering.utoronto.ca/research-innovation

Summer (or Full‑time) Research

USRA (NSERC)

  • Undergraduate Student Research Awards
  • Sponsored by NSERC.
  • 16 weeks of research supervised by a faculty member.
  • Eligibility: Canadian citizens or PR only.

UTEA (U of T Excellence)

  • University of Toronto Excellence Awards
  • Sponsored by U of T.
  • 14 full weeks of summer research.
  • Valued at a minimum of $7,500.
  • Use the DiscoverResearch tool to find professors.

Global Research Experiences

Summer Research Exchange

  • Guaranteed funding of up to $3,000.
  • Administered through the Centre for International Experience (CIE).
  • Gain engineering research experience abroad at world-class institutions.
  • 4-month internship for 2nd and 3rd-year students.

APS299Y

  • For-credit research-based summer course.
  • May count towards engineering program or engineering minor.

Summer Research Abroad

  • Open for upper-year undergrad students in science and engineering.
  • Projects available at partner institutions for about 12 weeks over the summer.
